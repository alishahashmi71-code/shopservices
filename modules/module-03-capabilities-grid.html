Shop Capabilities

Services Built Into Every Shop

From pressure testing to identification and kitting, Applied® hose shops are equipped to handle the full assembly lifecycle under one roof.

Up to 50,000 psi

Hydraulic Pressure Testing

Every hydraulic assembly is tested before it leaves the shop to confirm it will hold rated pressure under load. In-house capability supports assemblies up to 50,000 psi for the most demanding systems.

Up to 1,500 psi

Industrial Pressure Testing

Industrial hose assemblies are pressure tested to confirm integrity across chemical, steam, washdown, and material handling applications before they are installed in your operation.

Tagging

Each finished assembly can be labeled with identifying information including part number, build date, pressure rating, or custom data, so it remains traceable through its entire service life.

Laser Engraving

Permanent identification engraved directly onto fittings or metal tags. Resistant to washdown, chemicals, abrasion, and heat so assembly ID stays readable in harsh operating environments.

Clean and Cap

Assembly interiors are cleaned and hose ends capped before delivery to keep dust, debris, and contaminants out, critical for hydraulic systems where foreign particles cause component damage.

Pin Pricking

Small vent holes pricked through the hose cover allow trapped gas to escape in high-pressure gas service applications, preventing cover blistering and extending assembly service life.

Kitting

Assemblies prepared as installation-ready kits with everything needed grouped by project, line, or task. Reduces time on the floor and simplifies maintenance planning and material staging.

Industry Certified

NAHAD Certified Fabricators

Shop technicians trained and certified by the National Association of Hose and Accessories Distributors on current fabrication and assembly standards, so every build follows industry best practices.

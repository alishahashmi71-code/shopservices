How It Works

From Spec to Ship

Every assembly moves through four consistent stages, so quality and traceability carry through to the shop floor and into your operation.

STEP 01

Spec & Design

Our team works with you to match hose construction, fittings, pressure rating, and routing to the application. Spec sheets and drawings are reviewed before fabrication begins.

STEP 02

Fabricate

NAHAD certified fabricators build the assembly to spec using stocked brand materials and fittings. Every build follows current industry standards for cut, crimp, and assembly.

STEP 03

Test & ID

Assemblies are pressure tested to confirm integrity, then tagged or laser engraved with the identifying information you need for traceability over the product’s service life.

STEP 04

Clean, Kit & Ship

Finished assemblies are cleaned and capped to keep contamination out, then grouped as installation-ready kits if needed and delivered back to your operation.
